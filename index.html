WELCOME TO THE CONTROL CENTRE

A calmer road starts upstream.

Vehicles travel upwards through seven gantries along 10 km of motorway. The full motorway and all seven gantries are visible together. Incidents have orange rings and labelled callouts between gantries. Vehicle symbols stay the same size. Tap Detail, then choose a gantry or point on the road to inspect. Use Upstream/Downstream to move the close-up; Show all returns to the network. Tap an individual overhead sign to edit that lane directly on the road. Tap the gantry’s side label to set all three lanes. Tap elsewhere on the road to dismiss the controls. Changes apply immediately.

↖ / ↗ Merge: asks drivers to leave a lane. × Closed: vehicles merge or stop before the controlled section. Speed limits show mph.

For an incident, use a merge arrow at the gantry before it, then close the affected lane. Use the traffic desk to spot an approaching queue. Lower limits at the gantry before its tail to smooth arrivals, and watch hard braking, stopped vehicles and network flow. Suggested limits require your tap and never alter lane closures. Random incidents arrive at the traffic desk. Breakdowns always block the left lane; look for a stopped vehicle with flashing hazards. A recovery truck must reach each breakdown and load the vehicle before the lane clears. Recovery is dispatched automatically, and loading takes only a few seconds. Shed loads and pile-ups can block two lanes at once. Set arrows and closures upstream of both lanes; the crew clears the scene automatically. Debris and collisions take longer to clear. Reopen your signals once the obstruction is gone.

Flow counts vehicles leaving the motorway over the last simulated minute. Demand, headways, braking, lane changes, HGVs and imperfect compliance shape the result. This is an illustrative model, not an engineering prediction.

Police and ambulances target up to 110 and 100 mph respectively on clear roads; traffic yields when safe. Tap Sound on to hear sirens. Recovery uses the hard shoulder.

Tap J1–J3 for slip-road controls. Roads are normally Open; Closed holds traffic for an emergency. Drivers join lane 1 when a safe gap opens. Demand surges arrive automatically. Prepare roadwork closures before crews enter, and reopen them after completion. Police rolling holds create a debris-clearance gap, then release themselves. Network operations and optional shift goals are below the traffic desk.

There is no losing. Pause, experiment, and find a rhythm.
