WELCOME TO THE CONTROL CENTRE

A calmer road starts upstream.

Vehicles travel upwards through five gantries along 6 km of motorway. Tap any gantry on the motorway to open its controls. Set each lane’s speed or signal, then tap Done to return to the road. Changes apply immediately.

↖ / ↗ Merge: asks drivers to leave a lane. × Closed: vehicles merge or stop before the controlled section. Speed limits show mph.

For an incident, use a merge arrow at the gantry before it, then close the affected lane. Lower upstream speeds to smooth queues. Random incidents arrive at the traffic desk. Breakdowns always block the left lane; look for a stopped vehicle with flashing hazards. Recovery crews clear incidents automatically; reopen your signals once the obstruction is gone. You can also clear incidents early.

Flow counts vehicles leaving the motorway over the last simulated minute. Demand, headways, braking, lane changes, HGVs and imperfect compliance shape the result. This is an illustrative model, not an engineering prediction.

There is no losing. Pause, experiment, and find a rhythm.
