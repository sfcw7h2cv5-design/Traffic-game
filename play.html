WELCOME TO THE CONTROL CENTRE

A calmer road starts upstream.

Vehicles travel upwards through five gantries along 6 km of motorway. The full motorway and all five gantries are visible together. Incidents have orange rings and labelled callouts between gantries. Vehicle symbols stay the same size. Tap Detail for an optional close-up of the selected gantry, then Show all to return. Tap an individual overhead sign to edit that lane directly on the road. Tap the gantry’s side label to set all three lanes. Tap elsewhere on the road to dismiss the controls. Changes apply immediately.

↖ / ↗ Merge: asks drivers to leave a lane. × Closed: vehicles merge or stop before the controlled section. Speed limits show mph.

For an incident, use a merge arrow at the gantry before it, then close the affected lane. Lower upstream speeds to smooth queues. Random incidents arrive at the traffic desk. Breakdowns always block the left lane; look for a stopped vehicle with flashing hazards. A recovery truck must reach each breakdown and load the vehicle before the lane clears. Recovery is dispatched automatically, and loading takes only a few seconds. Debris and collisions take longer to clear. Reopen your signals once the obstruction is gone.

Flow counts vehicles leaving the motorway over the last simulated minute. Demand, headways, braking, lane changes, HGVs and imperfect compliance shape the result. This is an illustrative model, not an engineering prediction.

Police and ambulances use flashing blue lights and priority driving; traffic yields when safe. Tap Sound on to hear sirens. Recovery uses the hard shoulder.

There is no losing. Pause, experiment, and find a rhythm.
